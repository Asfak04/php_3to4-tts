Borrowing & Fine Logic

The system automates the most tedious parts of library management:

  • Standard Issued Period: 1 Month from the checkout date.
  • The Grace Period: 3-day window allowed after the due date with zero penalty.
  • Monetary Penalties: Fines accrue at a rate of 10 per day starting from the 4th overdue day.
  • Renewal Policy: One-time 1-month extension per book, subject to admin approval.
  • Cooldown Period: Students must wait 30 days after returning a title before re-issuing the same book copies.

Smart Reservations

A sophisticated waitlist ensures fair access to high-demand books. When a student reserves an out-of-stock book, the system handles the "Ready for Pickup" handshake automatically once the book is returned, locking it for that specific student for **3 days**.
